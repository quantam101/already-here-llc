## Identify a Low‑Maintenance Digital Asset You Can Sell Repeatedly  

Before you spend time building a funnel, you need a product that requires minimal ongoing creation but can be sold again and again.  

**Decision criteria**  
- **Skill match** – Choose something you already know or can learn in a few days (e.g., a template, a short video course, a subscription‑based resource library).  
- **Delivery simplicity** – The product should be downloadable or hosted on a platform that automates access (e.g., Gumroad, Podia, or a private WordPress membership area).  
- **Renewal potential** – Look for a problem that recurs monthly or quarterly (e.g., a content calendar, a design asset pack, a habit‑tracking spreadsheet).  

**Example (hypothetical)**  
Assume you are comfortable with graphic design. You decide to create a “Monthly Social Media Asset Pack” that includes 30 ready‑to‑post graphics for small business owners. The pack is updated each month, so customers have a reason to stay subscribed.

## Build a Simple, Automated Sales Funnel  

A funnel that runs on autopilot is the backbone of any recurring‑income system. The goal is to move a visitor from awareness to a paying subscriber with as few manual steps as possible.  

### 1. Capture Leads with a Value‑First Offer  
- Create a free “Mini Pack” (5 graphics) that solves a tiny, immediate need.  
- Place a sign‑up form on a dedicated landing page.  
- Use an email service that supports automation (e.g., ConvertKit, MailerLite).  

### 2. Nurture Leads Over a 3‑Day Sequence  
| Day | Email content | Purpose |
|-----|---------------|---------|
| 1   | Thank‑you + download link | Deliver promised free asset |
| 2   | Case study or testimonial | Show real‑world impact |
| 3   | Limited‑time discount for the full monthly pack | Create urgency |

### 3. Convert to a Recurring Subscription  
- Link the discount email to a checkout page that sets up a monthly recurring payment.  
- Offer two pricing tiers: **Basic** (monthly pack only) and **Premium** (monthly pack + quarterly live Q&A).  

### 4. Post‑Purchase Automation  
- Immediately email the subscriber their first month’s pack.  
- Add them to a “members‑only” area where future packs are uploaded.  
- Set a reminder email 2 days before the next month’s pack is released to keep engagement high.  

## Automate Delivery and Customer Management  

Even a simple product can become a time sink if you handle each transaction manually.  

- **Payment processor**: Choose a service that supports subscription billing and webhooks (e.g., Stripe).  
- **File hosting**: Store assets in a cloud folder (Google Drive, Dropbox) and generate shareable links that expire after a set period.  
- **Membership platform**: Use a lightweight plugin (MemberPress, Restrict Content Pro) that automatically grants access when Stripe confirms payment.  

**Automation checklist**  
- [ ] Connect Stripe to your membership plugin via webhook.  
- [ ] Set up a “new subscriber” trigger that sends the first pack and adds the email to the “Premium” segment if applicable.  
- [ ] Create a scheduled task (Zapier or Make) that uploads the next month’s pack to the members area and notifies subscribers.  

## Weekly Operating Routine  

Treat the business like a small operation with a fixed set of tasks each week. Consistency prevents churn and keeps the funnel healthy.  

| Day | Task | Time required | Why it matters |
|-----|------|---------------|----------------|
| Monday | Review subscription metrics (new sign‑ups, churn, revenue) | 30 min | Spot trends early |
| Tuesday | Create or curate next month’s digital asset pack | 2‑3 hrs | Fresh content fuels retention |
| Wednesday | Record a short “behind‑the‑scenes” video for Premium members | 45 min | Increases perceived value |
| Thursday | Run a 15‑minute QA session in the members forum (or reply to emails) | 30 min | Reduces support tickets |
| Friday | Update the landing page copy based on A/B test results | 1 hr | Improves conversion rates |
| Saturday | Light promotion: share a teaser on social media or a niche forum | 30 min | Keeps the funnel top‑of‑mind |
| Sunday | Rest / strategic planning | – | Prevents burnout |

**Tip:** Block the same calendar slots each week. Treat them as non‑negotiable appointments.

## Key Metrics to Track for Sustainable Recurring Income  

Understanding the numbers lets you adjust the funnel before problems become revenue leaks.  

- **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)** – Total subscription income per month.  
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)** – Total spend on ads, tools, and labor divided by the number of new subscribers that month.  
- **Churn Rate** – Percentage of subscribers who cancel each month. Aim for under 5 % for a healthy niche product.  
- **Lifetime Value (LTV)** – MRR × average subscription length (in months). Compare LTV to CAC; a ratio above 3:1 is a common benchmark.  
- **Conversion Rate (CR)** – Leads who become paying subscribers. Track both the lead‑capture page CR and the email‑sequence CR.  

**Example calculation (hypothetical)**  
Assumptions:  
- $15/month subscription price  
- 200 active subscribers  
- $300 spent on Facebook ads that month  
- 20 new subscribers acquired  

```
MRR = 200 × $15 = $3,000
CAC = $300 ÷ 20 = $15 per new subscriber
Churn = 5 cancellations ÷ 200 = 2.5%
LTV (average tenure 12 months) = $15 × 12 = $180
LTV:CAC ratio = $180 ÷ $15 = 12:1 (very healthy)
```

Use a simple spreadsheet to log these numbers weekly; the weekly routine above includes a 30‑minute review slot for exactly this purpose.

## Common Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them  

| Pitfall | Why it hurts | Mitigation |
|---------|--------------|------------|
| Over‑complicating the product | Increases creation time, raises price, reduces perceived value | Start with a single, well‑defined deliverable; add features only after you have a stable subscriber base. |
| Ignoring churn signals | Revenue drops silently | Set up automated alerts for cancellation emails; reach out with a quick “we miss you” survey. |
| Relying on a single traffic source | Platform policy changes can cut off leads | Diversify: combine organic social posts, a small ad budget, and occasional guest posts in niche newsletters. |
| Forgetting to renew assets | Subscribers feel abandoned, churn spikes | Use the weekly content creation slot; keep a backlog of at least two months’ worth of assets. |
| Skipping metric review | You can’t improve what you don’t measure | Treat the Monday review as a non‑negotiable KPI meeting. |

- [ ] Set up a free “lead magnet” landing page with an email capture form.  
- [ ] Build a 3‑day email nurture sequence that ends with a subscription offer.  
- [ ] Connect Stripe (or another processor) to a membership plugin and test the webhook flow.  
- [ ] Schedule the weekly routine in your calendar, blocking each task’s time slot.  
- [ ] Create a simple spreadsheet to record MRR, CAC, churn, LTV, and conversion rates.
