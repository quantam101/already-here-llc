## Current schedule

- Advanced engineering / smart hands: **$150/hour**, two-hour/**$300 minimum**.
- Same-day priority: **$175/hour**, two-hour/**$350 minimum**.
- After-hours / weekend: **$187.50–$200/hour**, two-hour minimum.
- Emergency response: **$225/hour**, two-hour/**$450 minimum**, with an under-four-hour target subject to availability.
- Standardized site audit: **$1,500**.
- Materials: **cost + 20%**.
- Priority support: **$2,000/month** for approximately **8–10 scheduled support hours**; additional work billed separately.

Mileage, extended-distance travel, parking, and tolls require written approval.
Net 15 is the target; Net 30 is the maximum without owner approval.

## What a good scope answers

1. What work is included and excluded?
2. What evidence or deliverable closes the job?
3. What site access, parts, tools, and remote support are required?
4. What rate class and minimum apply?
5. What happens if the scope changes onsite?

Submit the site, schedule, access, and closeout requirements through
[dispatch intake](/dispatch). No work is accepted until scope, availability,
and commercial terms are confirmed in writing.
