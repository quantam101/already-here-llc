# Pricing Analysis — AZ-MVS Asset Inventory & 2D QR Tagging

## What we are pricing

A turnkey baseline inventory and 2D QR tagging project across **24 physical site visits** in the Phoenix metro area. Scope includes wall-to-wall inventory, tag application, data capture, reconciliation, exception reporting, and final asset register delivery.

The RFP does not give a guaranteed asset count, so the proposal is built from:

1. A **per-site visit** labor/travel rate.
2. A **per-tag** material price that adjusts to the actual count.
3. Fixed project management and data-processing fees.

## Why this is competitive and profitable

1. **It is not a race-to-the-bottom quote.** At $17,800 we are cheaper than a $140–$180/hr small-business MSP for the same 96+ hours of work, and below the $20,000–$45,000 a national fixed-asset inventory firm would likely quote for 24 dispersed sites. The $2,200 return-visit/contingency allowance also covers the extras larger firms typically bill as change orders.
2. **It aligns with our own website.** The inventory/tagging rate of $450/visit sits inside the published Site Survey ($250–$500) and New Store/Site Survey ($350+) ranges. The additional $1,675 fixed mobilization recovery keeps the blended rate competitive for a small operator that must buy gear to execute.
3. **Per-tag cost is fair.** $1.25 covers a durable polyester QR label, printing, handling, and a small contingency for tag replacement or damaged assets. Bulk tag-only material pricing is ~$0.25–$0.38, so the per-tag price also helps absorb the extra labor for hard-to-reach or high-touch assets.
4. **The fixed PM and data-processing fees protect us.** $1,500 for kickoff/coordination and $750 for final reconciliation/QA are real hours that do not scale with tag count.
5. **It leaves room for a follow-on retainer.** The optional annual re-inventory ($300/site × 24 = $7,200/year) and hosted platform ($100/month) become recurring revenue without requiring us to underbid the first job.

---

## What we are *not* doing

- We are not using the $20K–$45K large-firm quotes as our price anchor; we are pricing below the bottom of that range while remaining profitable for a small operator.
- We are not using the cheapest tag-only material price as our total. Labor, travel, reconciliation, and project management are the real costs.

## Internal cost breakdown and margin (internal use only)

This is a small-business cost estimate so you can see what the $17,800 proposal actually has to cover. It assumes **one technician** completing 12 two-site days, plus owner/PM time for kickoff and final QA, and sets aside roughly half of the $2,200 return-visit/contingency allowance for likely re-visits or reschedules. The equipment line uses the midpoint of the one-time purchase range; use the low or high end to see the margin range.

| Cost category | Calculation | Estimated cost |
|---|---|---|
| On-site labor | 24 visits × 4 hrs × $40/hr loaded tech cost | $3,840 |
| Travel/drive time | 12 days × ~1.5 hrs × $40/hr | $720 |
| Mileage reimbursement | 647.4 miles × $0.67/mile | $434 |
| Tag consumables | 700 tags (labels + ribbon amortization) | ~$57 |
| Field equipment / supplies | Printer, scanner, labels, ribbon, case, power bank, misc (midpoint) | ~$1,300 |
| Project management / QA labor | Kickoff, scheduling, reconciliation, client coordination | ~$1,200 |
| Return-visit / reschedule cost reserve | Half of the $2,200 contingency allowance | ~$1,100 |
| **Estimated total direct cost** | | **~$8,651** |
| **Proposal revenue** | | **$17,800** |
| **Estimated gross margin** | | **~$9,149 (~51.4%)** |

### What this tells us

- Even after paying the technician, mileage, materials, full equipment purchase, PM time, and a reserve for return visits/reschedules, **the proposal leaves roughly $9,100 gross margin** before taxes, insurance, and overhead.
- The $450/site inventory rate plus $1,675 fixed mobilization and $2,200 contingency more than covers the direct labor, mileage, tag, and equipment cost.
- The $1,675 mobilization/equipment-recovery line is recovered by the client; if this job wins, the printer and scanner are already paid for, making future tagging jobs much more profitable.
- If two technicians are used or sites take longer than 4 hours, margin shrinks but stays healthy unless every visit runs 6+ hours.
