# Pricing Analysis — AZ-MVS Asset Inventory & 2D QR Tagging

## What we are pricing

A turnkey baseline inventory and 2D QR tagging project across **24 physical site visits** in the Phoenix metro area. Scope includes wall-to-wall inventory, tag application, data capture, reconciliation, exception reporting, and final asset register delivery.

The RFP does not give a guaranteed asset count, so the proposal is built from:

1. A **per-site visit** labor/travel rate.
2. A **per-tag** material price that adjusts to the actual count.
3. Fixed project management and data-processing fees.

## Small / local competitor benchmarks

We intentionally did not benchmark against national fixed-asset inventory firms with hundreds of technicians. Those economies of scale do not match a small Phoenix-area operator. Instead, we looked at local small-business IT pricing:

| Source | Rate / Package | What it tells us |
|---|---|---|
| **Expert IT Solutions** (Phoenix, itguyaz.com) | $20 service call + $124.99/hr on-site; $99.99/hr with contract | A one-person/small-team Phoenix shop charges ~$100–$125/hr for on-site work. |
| **RB Balch IT Services** (Phoenix, rbbalch.com) | $175/hr for small/medium businesses | Established small shop in the same service area. |
| **Turning Point IT** (Phoenix, turningpointit.com) | 10-hour bucket $1,800; 20-hour bucket $3,200; 40-hour bucket $5,600 | Effective hourly $140–$180 for small-business MSP on-site support. |
| **ASET Arizona state catalog** | Desktop/Server technician $85–$95/hr | Government-contracted baseline; real market rates for small business are higher. |
| **itemit QR tags** (UK, itemit.com) | 500 tags ≈ $190; 1,000 tags ≈ $255 | Material-only cost of ~$0.25–$0.38 per vinyl QR tag in bulk. |

**Effective hourly of our proposal:**

$450/site ÷ ~4 hrs = **$112.50/hr**.

That is between the state-contract baseline and the small MSP bucket rates, and below the $140–$180/hr Turning Point charges. It is a fair small-business rate that does not leave money on the table.

## Why this is competitive and profitable

1. **It is not a race-to-the-bottom quote.** At $13,600 we are cheaper than a $140–$180/hr MSP would be for the same 96+ hours of work, but more realistic than a one-person mobile repair rate because this is project work, not break/fix.
2. **It aligns with our own website.** $450/visit is inside the published Site Survey ($250–$500) and New Store/Site Survey ($350+) ranges, and only $50 above our Smart Hands Half-Day.
3. **Per-tag cost is fair.** $0.75 covers a durable polyester QR label, printing, and handling. Bulk tag-only pricing is ~$0.25–$0.38, so we are not gouging on material.
4. **The fixed PM and data-processing fees protect us.** $1,500 for kickoff/coordination and $750 for final reconciliation/QA are real hours that do not scale with tag count.
5. **It leaves room for a follow-on retainer.** The optional annual re-inventory ($300/site × 24 = $7,200/year) and hosted platform ($100/month) become recurring revenue without requiring us to underbid the first job.

---

## What we are *not* doing

- We are not matching the $3–$15/asset rates national fixed-asset inventory firms quote for 10,000+ asset facilities. Those rates rely on volume and cannot cover 24 dispersed Phoenix metro sites.
- We are not using the cheapest tag-only material price as our total. Labor, travel, reconciliation, and project management are the real costs.
