# AZ-MVS Asset Inventory & 2D QR Tagging — Executive Summary

Prepared for: Jeff Pomeroy, Arizona Multi-Site (AZ-MVS)  
Prepared by: Stephen Franklin, Already Here LLC  
Date: August 11, 2026

## Scope at a glance
- **24 physical site visits** across the Greater Phoenix area
- **~700 electronics assets** to inventory, tag, and reconcile
- **Deliverables:** master asset register, location-level files, exception report, site completion certifications, photo references

## Why we landed on $17,800

| Benchmark | What they would likely charge | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Local small-business IT service rates | $100–$180/hr on-site | At 96+ field hours, a straight hourly job would cost $10,000–$17,000+ before travel and tagging. |
| National fixed-asset inventory firms (CPCON / large inventory providers) | $20,000–$45,000 for 24 dispersed sites / ~700 assets | High per-site minimums, longer timelines, and return-visit/complication fees often billed as change orders. |
| **Our proposal** | **$17,800** | Sits just below the national floor while covering the real-world extras larger firms add on. |

We split the difference between the original lean small-business price ($15,600) and the large-firm floor ($20,000) to build in room for the realities of a multi-site rollout.

## How the $17,800 is built

| Component | Amount |
|---|---|
| Project management / kickoff | $1,500 |
| Onsite inventory & tagging (24 visits × $450) | $10,800 |
| Mobilization & first-job equipment recovery | $1,675 |
| Asset tags (≈700 × $1.25) | ~$875 |
| Data capture & final reconciliation | $750 |
| **Return visits, complications & reschedule allowance** | **$2,200** |
| Photos, travel / mileage | Included |
| **TOTAL ONE-TIME PROJECT PRICE** | **$17,800** |

- Not-to-exceed: **$18,500**
- All-in per tagged asset: **~$25.43** at 700 assets
- Per-tag pricing stays at **$1.25** (actual count)

## What the $2,200 contingency covers
This allowance replaces the change-order approach larger firms use. It handles:
- Sites that need a second pass due to access issues or missing serials
- Reschedules caused by construction, pre-opening delays, or site contact no-shows
- Extra technician time and mileage for re-visits

## Margin
Estimated direct cost, including labor, mileage, tags, equipment, PM/QA, and a reserve for likely re-visits: **~$8,650**.  
Estimated gross margin at $17,800: **~$9,150 (~51%)**.

## Bottom line
$17,800 is competitive with — and slightly below — what a national inventory firm would charge for the same scope, while protecting Already Here from the return-visit and reschedule costs that turn lean bids into unprofitable ones.
