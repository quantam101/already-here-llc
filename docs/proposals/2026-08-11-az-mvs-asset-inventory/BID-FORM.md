## Pricing Notes

- Site-visit pricing includes travel within the Phoenix metropolitan area. Sites beyond a 60-mile one-way drive from downtown Phoenix may incur additional mileage at IRS/standard business rates, quoted before scheduling.
- The per-tag rate covers durable 2D QR polyester labels (≈1" × 2") with human-readable asset IDs, suitable for normal indoor commercial use, applied by Already Here field technicians.
- Data capture and reconciliation includes on-device barcode/QR capture, nightly spreadsheet sync, duplicate-ID checks, missing-serial review, photo linking, and final export processing.
- Photos are included for assets over $500 replacement value or any asset specifically requested by Client; one photo per qualifying asset, filename keyed to Asset ID.
