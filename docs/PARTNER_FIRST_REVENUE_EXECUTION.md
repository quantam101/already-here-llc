# Partner-First Revenue Execution

## Objective

Move Already Here LLC toward the $500/day target by prioritizing paid work and vendor relationships before cold-selling automation.

## Canonical order of operations

1. Repair any critical revenue-system blocker that prevents lead capture, collection, or attribution.
2. Pursue direct paid work Already Here LLC can perform immediately.
3. Pursue recurring vendor, overflow, subcontract, and field-service partnerships.
4. Use completed work to learn the customer's real workflow and document friction.
5. Sell automation against observed, quantified problems after trust and proof exist.
6. Use automation-first outreach only when there is no credible paid-work or vendor relationship path.

## Target-company treatment

### MSPs, POS companies, low-voltage, CCTV, access control, AV, data center, healthcare field service
Lead with field-service capacity: smart hands, break/fix, deployment, site surveys, equipment swaps, network/Wi-Fi support, cabling-adjacent technical work, after-hours coverage, project rollout support, and nationwide coordination.

### Property managers and real-estate operators
Lead with approved-vendor / overflow support for IT, networking, Wi-Fi, CCTV, access control, smart devices, technical troubleshooting, and dispatch coordination. After jobs are completed, offer maintenance-intake, routing, vendor-dispatch, closeout, and follow-up automation.

### HVAC, plumbing, electrical, refrigeration
Do not compete in licensed trade work. Lead with technical support that fits Already Here LLC's scope: networking, Wi-Fi, POS, CCTV, access control, communications, smart-hands, device/site support, and back-office workflow automation after a relationship exists.

### Hauling, junk removal, mobile mechanics
Use partnership/referral opportunities where credible. Otherwise automation-first is acceptable because direct technical field-work overlap is narrower.

## Outreach doctrine

The default opening is vendor/partner-first, not an AI pitch:

- Ask whether they use subcontractors, overflow providers, smart-hands technicians, or project partners.
- State Already Here LLC's applicable field-service capabilities.
- Offer single-dispatch, project, recurring-coverage, and multi-site support.
- Mention automation only as a later expansion capability after workflow familiarity.

## Scoring dimensions

The partner-first policy scores candidates using:

- Immediate paid-work potential
- Speed to cash
- Recurring vendor potential
- Field-service fit
- Route-stacking fit
- Workflow-learning value
- Automation-expansion potential
- Attribution quality
- Low-margin risk
- Payment-enforcement risk

Critical revenue-system blockers can supersede selling when they prevent actual collection or attribution.

## Approval gates

Discovery, scoring, drafting, and local persistence can run automatically. The following remain approval-gated:

- sending email/SMS/outreach
- accepting work
- submitting vendor applications/forms
- moving money
- modifying credentials
- production deployment or production-file changes

## Revenue ladder

The preferred account progression is:

`paid dispatch/project → repeat vendor work → preferred-provider relationship/retainer → observed workflow evidence → automation implementation → recurring automation support`

This is the default strategy for future revenue scans and prospect ranking.
