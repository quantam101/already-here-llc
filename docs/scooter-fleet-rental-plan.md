# Master Business, Operations & Setup Plan: Scooter Fleet Rentals (A+)

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Model

**Company / Operations:** Already Here LLC — Turnkey Scooter Rentals for Gig Delivery Drivers  
**Fleet Size:** 3 scooters (Phase 1 launch fleet)  
**Primary Market:** Active gig delivery drivers (DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub, Instacart, Amazon Flex) and courier subcontractors in the Phoenix / Tempe / Scottsdale corridor.  
**Business Model:** Direct commercial asset rental (lessor-lessee, not subcontractor-platform).  

**Model Overview:**  
Rent street-legal, insured, commercially equipped scooters to active gig drivers on a recurring weekly or monthly subscription. Drivers pay in advance every Monday; Already Here LLC owns, registers, insures, and maintains the fleet. This is a simple asset-rental model: predictable recurring revenue, no shared-platform tax ambiguity, and minimal administrative overhead compared to subcontracting delivery platform accounts.

**Strategic thesis:**  
- Gig drivers need reliable, low-cost, high-MPG vehicles without the capital outlay or repair risk of ownership.  
- A 3-unit pilot validates unit economics with low capital at risk ($910).  
- A scooter rents for ~$155/week; payback on the upfront equipment package is under 1 month if all 3 units are rented.

## 4. Maintenance, Upkeep & Usage Policy

### Owner Responsibilities (Routine Wear-and-Tear)

- Engine oil changes every 1,000 miles or 30 days, whichever comes first.
- Standard tire tread and brake pad replacement due to normal wear.
- Routine battery and electrical system health checks.
- Mandatory free 15-minute inspection every 1,000 miles or 30 days.

### Renter Responsibilities (Operational Incidents & Expenses)

- Fuel (gasoline).
- Flat tire repairs / punctures sustained during daily operation.
- Damage from accidents, drops, crashes, or neglect.
- All parking, red-light, speed-camera, and moving violations.
- Tolls and impound fees.
- Returning scooter in same condition, less normal wear-and-tear.

## 6. Operational Risk & Mitigation (A+ Addition)

### Key Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Non-payment / mid-week default | Medium | High | Auto-billing Monday; card on file; remote GPS immobilization; deposit held |
| Accidents / liability | Medium | High | Commercial liability + garage/lessor insurance; enforce helmet use; signed waiver |
| Theft / total loss | Low | High | Hardwired GPS tracker; geofence alerts; renter liable for FMV/deductible |
| Mechanical abuse / neglect | Medium | Medium | Mandatory 30-day/1,000-mile inspection; deposit fee schedule |
| GPS tampering | Low | High | $150 penalty + immediate immobilization; tamper-evident wiring |
| Regulatory / licensing | Medium | Medium | Verify M-class endorsement, valid license, registration mirrors owner's insurance |
| Seasonal demand swings | Medium | Medium | Offer monthly discount; standby list; corporate courier overflow |

### Insurance & Legal

- **Commercial general liability** policy naming scooter rental as an activity.
- **Garage / lessor policy** or commercial auto coverage on fleet.
- Renter provides proof of valid driver's license with M endorsement.
- Contract includes hold-harmless, assumption of risk, and indemnity clauses.
- No scooter leaves without signed rental agreement, inspection checklist, and photos.

## 7. Customer Acquisition & Growth (A+ Addition)

### Launch Channels

- Craigslist "Services > Labor/Move" and "For Sale > Motorcycles/Scooters"
- Facebook Marketplace listings in Phoenix / Tempe / Scottsdale
- Local gig-driver Facebook groups and Discord servers
- Flyers at DoorDash / Uber Eats hotspots (restaurant clusters, apartment complexes)
- Referral bonus: $25 off next week for every referred driver who completes 4 paid weeks

### Sales Motion

1. Driver inquires via listing or website.
2. Pre-screen: active delivery app account, valid license, clean background (self-reported).
3. Schedule in-person inspection and contract signing.
4. Collect $305 onboarding charge.
5. Hand over keys, add-ons, and safety kit; activate GPS geofence.
6. Weekly auto-bill resumes every Monday until returned.

### Conversion KPIs

- Inquiry-to-inspection rate target: 30 %
- Inspection-to-rental rate target: 70 %
- Weekly churn target: < 10 %
- Fleet utilization target: > 85 %
- Add-on attachment rate target: > 40 %
