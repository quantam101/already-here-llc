# Field Closeout Checklist

Use this checklist before marking a job complete.

## Pre-work
- [ ] Customer authorization signed or recorded
- [ ] Site hazard assessment complete
- [ ] Required tools and parts on-site
- [ ] Before photographs captured

## During work
- [ ] Scope performed per work order
- [ ] Parts and materials logged with quantities and costs
- [ ] Testing results recorded

## Completion
- [ ] After / completion photographs captured
- [ ] Customer acceptance obtained
- [ ] Customer signature or digital approval received
- [ ] QA review passed (pass / needs review / fail)

## Billing
- [ ] Revenue amount confirmed
- [ ] Technician payout calculated
- [ ] Mileage and disposal costs logged
- [ ] Invoice or receipt sent
